Smell Test support

Smell Test helps you check a text, email or link that feels off before you tap, reply or pay.

Common questions

It says it can't check on this iPhone. The on-device check needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

What does "Not sure" mean? The app couldn't tell. Don't tap links or share anything yet. Contact the sender using a number or app you already trust, or ask for a second opinion.

Is "No red flags found" a guarantee? No. Smell Test can be wrong. If something feels off, trust that.

I already clicked, paid or shared something. Turn on "Already clicked, paid or shared?" on the result for recovery steps, and contact your bank or the real company using a number you already trust.
