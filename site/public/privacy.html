Smell Test privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Smell Test is made by Cognito Agora, Inc.

The short version

Smell Test checks messages on your device first: on your iPhone, or in desktop Chrome on your computer. It only sends anything to the cloud when you ask for a second opinion, and only after removing personal details and showing you exactly what will be sent. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Checks on your iPhone

When you paste a message, or share one from another app, the first check runs with Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. The message isn't sent to us or anyone else for this check, and Smell Test doesn't save it.

Screenshots

If you choose a screenshot, or share one to Smell Test, your iPhone reads the words in it using Apple's built-in text recognition. The picture itself is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and Smell Test doesn't save it. Smell Test can only see the picture you pick, not the rest of your photos. The words it reads are then treated exactly like a pasted message.

The Chrome version

Smell Test also runs as a web page at iossmelltest.web.app/check. In desktop Chrome, the first check runs with Chrome's built-in AI model on your computer. The message isn't sent to us or anyone else for this check, and the page doesn't save it. The first time, Chrome may need to download its model from Google; Chrome handles that download, and it doesn't include your message.

If your browser can't check messages on its own, the page tells you so and only offers the second opinion described below, with personal details removed first.

Second opinion (only if you choose it)

If you tap or click "Get a second opinion", Smell Test first removes names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, account, card and Social Security numbers, and verification codes from the message. For links it keeps only the website name, which is the strongest scam sign. It then shows you the result, and you can edit it. Only if you agree is that edited text sent, through Google's Firebase AI Logic, to Google's Gemini model, which sends back an answer.

The automatic removal can miss things. Check the preview before you send.

Google handles this text under its own terms. While Smell Test uses Google's free Gemini API tier, Google uses the text it receives to provide and improve its products, and human reviewers at Google may read it. That's another reason personal details are removed first. See Google's Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. Cognito Agora doesn't store this text.

Other things the app sends

  • App Check: before a second opinion, the app uses Apple's App Attest to prove to Firebase that the request comes from the real Smell Test app. This doesn't include your message.
  • App Check on the web: the Chrome version uses Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise instead, to show Firebase the request comes from the real Smell Test page and not a bot. reCAPTCHA collects information about your browser and device and sends it to Google for this purpose, under Google's privacy policy and terms. This doesn't include your message.
  • Settings: the app and the web page download their settings from Firebase Remote Config. To do this, Firebase uses an installation identifier that isn't linked to you. On the web, this identifier and the App Check token are kept in your browser's storage for this site.

What we don't do

No accounts or sign-in. No advertising. No analytics. No selling or sharing of your data. No tracking across apps or websites. (reCAPTCHA on the Chrome version is used only to block abuse, as described above.)

Age

Smell Test is for adults 18 and over. Google's Gemini terms, which apply to the second opinion, require users to be 18 or older. Smell Test isn't directed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
